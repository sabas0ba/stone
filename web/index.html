stone

From a hand-encoded 256-byte seed to an assembler, a C compiler, a libc and a homemade OS — one generation at a time. Each stage is built only from the artifacts of the previous stage, and the whole process is bit-reproducible.

Every artifact on this page is a genuine chain product, and runs right here in your browser on a RV32 emulator.
